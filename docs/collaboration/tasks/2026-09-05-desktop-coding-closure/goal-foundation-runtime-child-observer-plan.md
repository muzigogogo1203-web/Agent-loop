## Decision and stop

The microstep ends after self-check and at most one complete workload plus independent evidence analysis. Queued bytes before delayed first-read support a reader-service interval; repeated valid zero-byte samples plus nonprogressing child counters/run state support a child-side interval only over sampled instants. Neither proves the exact loader/security cause. SIGCONT success does not itself prove resumed state, and BSD/thread state does not supply a Mach suspend count. Positive helper capability is not test success; a GREEN workload is non-reproduction, not a repair. Any unavailable capability, missed target, identity mismatch, sample truncation or unexplained failure remains explicit. A fast passing child may live for less than100ms and be missed completely; this is unavailable coverage for that target, not permission to infer its states or rerun. It does not erase independently joined valid samples of another target. A missed failing target leaves its cause unresolved. Select a fix only from new evidence; otherwise state the precise remaining unknown rather than repeating the run. A1 acceptance/A2 entry remain closed.
